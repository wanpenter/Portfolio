Overview

VaultBreak MR is a mixed reality stealth game built for Meta Quest 3S. The player's real room becomes a laser-protected vault through passthrough and room-aware route generation.

Sony Mocopi tracks the player's body so crouching, leaning, and stepping around lasers become part of the gameplay. Players can also shoot control boxes to disable selected laser sections.

The objective is simple: survive the security route, open the safebox, and grab the pearl before health reaches zero.

Outcome

The completed prototype combined Meta Quest passthrough, Sony Mocopi full-body tracking, and physical laser-dodging gameplay. Testing with 20 participants showed an increase in reported interaction confidence, while also highlighting calibration and usability as the main areas for improvement.
